# Concept Exploration

> Working directions, not final decisions.

## World Foundation

| Dimension | Current Direction |
|---|---|
| World | Living procedural world, not a realistic Earth simulation |
| Environment | Terrain, water, vegetation |
| Life | Individuals + groups |
| Civilization | Architecture, settlements, social relationships |
| Behavior | Movement, interaction, traces |
| Visual | Stylized 3D + graphic / abstract elements |
| Goal | Beautiful from afar, interesting when inspected closely |

# Concept Directions

| Direction | Core Question | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Living Atlas** | What if a map were alive? | World as both map and landscape |
| **Emergent Civilization** | What if civilization grew from world conditions? | Architecture, settlement, environment |
| **Strange Ecology** | What if life felt alive without resembling Earth? | Organisms, behavior, ecology |
| **Inverted Ecology** | What if familiar ecological relationships were reversed? | Food chains, agency, environment |

## Living Atlas

**Map ↔ World**

Terrain, organisms, vegetation, settlements, paths, and territories form a graphic composition while representing systems that actually exist in 3D.

## Emergent Civilization

```text
terrain / resources
        ↓
     organisms
        ↓
 movement + paths
        ↓
   settlements
        ↓
   architecture
```

Buildings should have reasons for their **location, form, and relationships**.

## Strange Ecology

Explore life through a mix of:

`recognizable` + `alien` + `geometric` + `illustrative`

Individuals matter, but also form groups, interact, move, and leave traces.

## Inverted Ecology

Possible inversions:

| Familiar | Possible Inversion |
|---|---|
| animals eat plants | plants prey on animals |
| large dominates small | small controls large |
| civilization controls vegetation | vegetation shapes civilization |
| environment supports organisms | organisms support environment |
| architecture removes nature | architecture adapts to nature |

The inversion needs an internal logic, not just visual strangeness.

# Visual Preferences

### Interested in

`bold contrast` · `strong forms` · `curves` · `stylized 3D` · `architecture` · `graphic details` · `cartographic qualities` · `controlled color`

Typography:

`typewriter-like labels` × `clean sans-serif`

### Less Interested in

`photorealism` · `futuristic` · `machine-like` · `cute` · `overly playful` · `strongly dreamy`

### Desired Reaction

**Beautiful**
→ **Zoom in**
→ **Inspect**
→ **Discover relationships**
→ **Understand the world differently**

# Exploration Process

```text
CONCEPT
   ↓
COLLECT
5–10 references
   ↓
ASK
What exists?
What relates?
What changes?
   ↓
SKETCH
2D / diagram / Figma / simple 3D
   ↓
TEST
one procedural rule
   ↓
COMPARE
keep / combine / discard
```
